🎭 Deepfake Scam Awareness

You receive a voice note that sounds like your relative asking urgent money transfer.

📌 How this scam occurs

Deepfake scams use artificial intelligence to create fake voice or video messages that look or sound real. Fraudsters pretend to be family members, bosses, or friends and ask for urgent money.

✅ Safety steps

  • Verify the request by calling the real person directly.
  • Use another communication channel for confirmation.
  • Be careful with urgent emotional requests.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not send money based only on voice/video messages.
  • Do not trust urgent pressure without verification.
