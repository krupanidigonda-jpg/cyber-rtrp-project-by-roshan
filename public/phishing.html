🔗 Phishing Link Detector

You receive this link in SMS:

www.paytm-secure-login-check.in

📌 How this scam occurs

Phishing scams happen when attackers send fake links through SMS, email, or social media. These links look similar to official websites such as banks, payment apps, or shopping sites. When the user clicks the link and enters login details, the attacker steals that information.

✅ Safety steps

  • Check the website URL carefully before opening it.
  • Use official apps or type the website manually in browser.
  • Look for spelling mistakes or unusual domain names.
  • Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not click unknown links sent in SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Do not enter passwords on suspicious websites.
  • Do not trust urgent messages asking for login or payment.
