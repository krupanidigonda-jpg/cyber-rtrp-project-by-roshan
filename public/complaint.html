Cyber Crime Complaint Help

Emergency Helpline

Call 1930 immediately if money fraud happened.

Official Reporting Website

Visit: https://cybercrime.gov.in

If Fake Police / Fake WhatsApp ID

  • Do not send money
  • Verify from official police website
  • Take screenshot of profile
  • Report in cybercrime portal

If Photo Morphing / Blackmail

  • Do not panic
  • Do not pay money
  • Save screenshots as evidence
  • Block the person
  • Report immediately

If Spam Call / Bank Fraud

  • Never share OTP
  • Never share ATM PIN
  • Verify with official bank number
  • Report suspicious number
