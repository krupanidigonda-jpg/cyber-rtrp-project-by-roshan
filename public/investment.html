💰 Investment Scam Awareness

A Telegram group says: “Invest ₹5000 today and get ₹50,000 tomorrow.”

📌 How this scam occurs

Investment scams promise huge returns in a very short time. Fraudsters use fake websites, Telegram groups, and social media pages to attract users. Once money is invested, the scammer disappears or blocks the victim.

✅ Safety steps

  • Verify the platform before investing.
  • Use only trusted financial institutions.
  • Be careful with guaranteed profit claims.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not trust unrealistic high returns.
  • Do not send money to unknown accounts.
  • Do not join unknown investment groups blindly.
