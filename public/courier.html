📦 Courier Scam Detection

Message says: “Your parcel is stuck. Pay ₹49 now using this link.”

📌 How this scam occurs

Courier scams happen when victims receive messages saying a parcel is delayed or stuck. The message asks for a small payment through a suspicious link. That link usually steals banking details.

✅ Safety steps

  • Track delivery only through official courier websites/apps.
  • Verify tracking ID directly on official site.
  • Ignore unknown payment links.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not pay through random courier links.
  • Do not enter card details on suspicious pages.
