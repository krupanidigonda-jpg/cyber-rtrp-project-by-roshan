📡 SIM Swap Fraud

Your phone suddenly loses network and you get alerts of banking activity on email.

📌 How this scam occurs

In SIM swap fraud, attackers transfer the victim’s phone number to a new SIM card. Once they control the number, they receive OTPs and can access bank accounts and other services.

✅ Safety steps

  • Contact telecom operator immediately if network disappears unexpectedly.
  • Inform your bank and block suspicious transactions.
  • Enable strong account security measures.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not ignore sudden loss of network.
  • Do not delay contacting operator or bank.
