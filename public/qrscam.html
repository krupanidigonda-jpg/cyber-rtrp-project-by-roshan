📷 QR Code Scam Awareness

A person says: “Scan this QR code to receive ₹5000 refund.”

📌 How this scam occurs

In QR code scams, fraudsters tell victims that scanning a QR code will help them receive money, cashback, refund, or prize money. In reality, scanning a QR code is usually used to make a payment, so the victim may lose money.

✅ Safety steps

  • Scan QR codes only when you are making a payment yourself.
  • Verify the receiver name before completing payment.
  • Use trusted payment apps only.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not scan QR codes sent by unknown people.
  • Do not trust messages saying “scan to receive money”.
  • Do not enter UPI PIN unless you are sure you are paying.
