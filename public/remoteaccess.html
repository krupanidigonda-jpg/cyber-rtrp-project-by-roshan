🖥️ Remote Access App Scam

A caller asks you to install AnyDesk to “fix” your account problem.

📌 How this scam occurs

In remote access scams, fraudsters ask victims to install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer. Once installed, the scammer can see and control the victim’s screen, including banking and payment apps.

✅ Safety steps

  • Contact official customer support directly.
  • Uninstall remote apps if installed by mistake.
  • Use antivirus and device lock protection.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not install remote access apps for unknown callers.
  • Do not share screen while using bank apps.
