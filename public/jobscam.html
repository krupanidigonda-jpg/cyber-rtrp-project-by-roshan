💼 Fake Job Offer Checker

You get a message: “Work from home, earn ₹5000 daily. Pay ₹999 registration fee now.”

📌 How this scam occurs

Fake job scams happen through WhatsApp, Telegram, email, and social media. Fraudsters offer high salary jobs or work-from-home jobs and then ask for registration fees, training fees, or personal documents.

✅ Safety steps

  • Verify the company through official website or LinkedIn.
  • Check if the job is posted on trusted job portals.
  • Contact company HR using official contact details.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not pay money to get a job.
  • Do not share bank details or Aadhaar immediately.
  • Do not trust unrealistic salary promises.
