☎️ Fake Support Number Scam

You search online for customer care and call the first number shown in a random result.

📌 How this scam occurs

Fake support scams happen when scammers post false customer care numbers online. When victims call, the fraudster asks for OTP, bank details, or asks to install remote access apps.

✅ Safety steps

  • Check support numbers only from official websites/apps.
  • Never share OTP or PIN with support staff.
  • Use official chat or support center only.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not trust numbers from random search results.
  • Do not install apps suggested by unknown support agents.
