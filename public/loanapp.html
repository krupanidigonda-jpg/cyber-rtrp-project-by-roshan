📱 Fake Loan App Fraud

A loan app promises instant money and asks for full contact and gallery access.

📌 How this scam occurs

Fake loan apps attract victims by offering instant loans with very little documentation. After installation, they ask for access to contacts, photos, and files. Later, scammers misuse this data for harassment and blackmail.

✅ Safety steps

  • Install apps only from official app stores.
  • Check app reviews and developer details.
  • Read app permissions carefully.

❌ What not to do

  • Do not install unknown APK files.
  • Do not allow contact or gallery access unnecessarily.
  • Do not trust loan apps with no proper company details.
